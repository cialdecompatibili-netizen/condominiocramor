An online area to read documents, statements and notices and to report problems easily, also from a smartphone.

## What you find
- Digital notice board with minutes, statements and circulars
- Personal statement for each owner
- Fault reporting with photos
- Alerts for deadlines and meetings
- Always up-to-date document archive
- Access from computer and smartphone, for authorised users only

## How to access
If your building is managed by us, you receive personal credentials from the office. Access is reserved for authorised users.

## Security and privacy
Each owner only sees the data that concerns them. Data is handled in line with privacy law.

## FAQ
**Is a residents' area mandatory?**  
No, but it improves transparency and communication. Each owner only sees what concerns them.

**Is my data protected?**  
Access is personal and password-protected; data is handled in line with privacy law.
