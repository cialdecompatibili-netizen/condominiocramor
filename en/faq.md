Answers to the most frequent questions on administration, meetings, statements, defaulters and works in a building. For your specific case, write to us.

## [Ordinary condominium administration]({{ '/en/services/ordinary-condominium-administration/' | relative_url }})

### When is a manager mandatory?
When there are more than eight owners the owners' meeting must appoint one (art. 1129 of the Italian Civil Code). Below that it is optional, but often worthwhile.

### How long is the mandate?
One year, renewable for the same period. The fee must be itemised when the manager is appointed, otherwise the appointment is void.

## [Transparent accounting and annual statement]({{ '/en/services/transparent-accounting-and-annual-statement/' | relative_url }})

### Can I see the accounting documents?
Yes: owners can inspect the supporting documents and get copies at their own expense (art. 1130-bis of the Italian Civil Code).

### When must the statement be approved?
The manager must call the meeting to approve it within 180 days of the end of the financial year (art. 1130).

## [Owners' meetings, also online]({{ '/en/services/owners-meetings-also-online/' | relative_url }})

### How much notice is required?
The notice must be given at least five days before the meeting (art. 66 of the implementing provisions).

### Can the meeting be held online?
Under certain conditions yes, for example if the building rules allow it or the owners authorise it: we check each case.

## [Residents' area and online reporting]({{ '/en/services/residents-portal-and-app/' | relative_url }})

### Is a residents' area mandatory?
No, but it improves transparency and communication. Each owner only sees what concerns them.

### Is my data protected?
Access is personal and password-protected; data is handled in line with privacy law.

## [Fault reporting and emergency response]({{ '/en/services/fault-reporting-and-emergency-response/' | relative_url }})

### Can the manager order urgent works without a meeting?
Yes, for urgent extraordinary maintenance, but he or she must report it to the next meeting (art. 1135 of the Italian Civil Code).

### What do I do if there is a serious fault?
Report it straight away through the office channels, with a photo if possible: we activate the supplier and keep owners updated.

## [Unpaid dues recovery]({{ '/en/services/unpaid-dues-recovery/' | relative_url }})

### Can the manager act against defaulters without a meeting?
Yes: an immediately enforceable payment order can be obtained without the meeting's authorisation (art. 63 of the implementing provisions).

### What happens after six months of arrears?
The manager may suspend the defaulting owner from separable common services (art. 63 of the implementing provisions).

## [Maintenance, contracts and suppliers]({{ '/en/services/maintenance-contracts-and-suppliers/' | relative_url }})

### Who chooses the contractor?
The owners' meeting decides, on the manager's proposal after quotes have been collected and compared.

### Who supervises the works?
The manager follows the site and, when needed, relies on a licensed technician for site management and acceptance.

## [Extraordinary works and tax bonuses]({{ '/en/services/extraordinary-works-and-tax-bonuses/' | relative_url }})

### Are the deductions for building works always the same?
No: rates, limits and deadlines change. Before each resolution we check the current rules with a technician or an accountant.

### Are special majorities needed for extraordinary works?
It depends on the type of work: the meeting decides with the majorities set by art. 1136 of the Italian Civil Code and special rules for some works.

## [Safety, compliance and privacy]({{ '/en/services/safety-compliance-and-privacy/' | relative_url }})

### Who handles lift inspections?
Periodic checks must be assigned to licensed bodies: the manager schedules them and keeps the records.

### Can a camera be installed in the common areas?
Yes, with a meeting resolution (art. 1122-ter of the Italian Civil Code) and in line with privacy rules: we assess each case.

## [Changing manager and handover]({{ '/en/services/changing-manager-and-handover/' | relative_url }})

### Must the outgoing manager hand over the documents?
Yes: when the mandate ends he or she must hand over all documents and perform urgent tasks to avoid harm to owners (art. 1129 of the Italian Civil Code).

### How do we change manager?
The meeting can dismiss and appoint with the majorities set by law: we help you prepare the agenda and the quote to present.
