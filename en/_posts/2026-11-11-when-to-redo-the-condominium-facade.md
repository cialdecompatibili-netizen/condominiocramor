A facade is not redone for looks alone: it is the building's skin and protects structure and flats. Here is when to act.

## Signs not to ignore
- Plaster that sounds hollow, crumbles or detaches
- Cracks that widen or multiply
- Dark stains, mould and salt efflorescence
- Flaking or faded paint
- Debris fallen on the pavement

## How long does a facade last
It depends on materials, exposure and maintenance, but a periodic check every few years lets you act before damage gets serious.

## How to proceed
1. Inspection with photo report, drone included.
2. Itemised quote to present at the owners' meeting.
3. Choice between scaffolding and rope access, depending on the building.
4. Phased execution and final documentation.

The owners' meeting decides the right moment: with a clear report the decision is easier.
