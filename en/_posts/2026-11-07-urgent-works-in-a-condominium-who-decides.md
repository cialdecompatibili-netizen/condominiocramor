Leaks, unsafe cornices, broken systems: some things cannot wait for the next meeting.

## What the law says
The manager may order urgent extraordinary maintenance works, but must report to the next meeting (art. 1135 of the Italian Civil Code).

## In practice
1. **Report** the problem straight away, with photos.
2. **Secure** the area and activate the right supplier.
3. **Inform** owners about the status of the works.
4. **Report** to the meeting and document the costs.

## What counts as urgent
Something is urgent if it cannot wait for the meeting without causing harm to people, property or the building: a water leak, a cornice coming loose, an electrical or lift fault that endangers people.

## Who can decide
- The manager may order urgent extraordinary maintenance without a meeting but must report to the meeting right away (art. 1135 of the Civil Code). If the costs are significant, call it quickly.
- A single owner may advance the cost only if the work was truly urgent. Otherwise there is no right to reimbursement (art. 1134).

## What to do in practice
1. Make safe (shut off water, cordon off, disconnect the system).
2. Document with photos, date and time.
3. Call a technician and notify the insurer within three days (art. 1913), unless the policy says otherwise.
4. Have the expense ratified at the next meeting and shared by thousandths.

The line between urgent and postponable is the hard part: when in doubt, do the minimum to make safe and then hold a quick meeting for the rest.
