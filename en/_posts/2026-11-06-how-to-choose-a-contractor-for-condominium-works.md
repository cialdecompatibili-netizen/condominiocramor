Choosing the right contractor is the difference between a good job and years of disputes.

## Checklist
- **Several quotes**, compared item by item and not only on the total.
- **Company documents**: chamber of commerce registration, social-security certificate, insurance.
- **Written contract** describing the works, times, staged payments and guarantees.
- **Verifiable references** for similar works.
- **Site management** assigned, when needed, to a licensed technician.

## Who decides
The owners' meeting decides, on the manager's proposal.

## How to choose a contractor without regrets
1. **One specification**: give every contractor the same list of work so quotes are truly comparable.
2. **Check the documents**: company registration, DURC (contribution compliance), liability insurance and technical-professional suitability. The client, i.e. the building, must verify it (Legislative Decree 81/2008).
3. **Safety**: if several contractors work on site a safety coordinator is needed. Scaffolding and work at height need specific licences.
4. **Written contract**: price, timing, delay penalties, progress payments, a retention until completion testing and a site contact.
5. **Site supervision**: for works on facades, roof or structures it is wise to appoint a technician independent from the contractor.

## After the works
Defects must be reported within sixty days of discovery (art. 1667 of the Civil Code). For serious defects in buildings the contractor is liable for ten years (art. 1669). Keep certificates, site photos and declarations of conformity: you need them for warranties and tax bonuses.
