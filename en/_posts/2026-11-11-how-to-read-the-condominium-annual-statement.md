The annual statement consists of an accounts register, a financial summary and an explanatory note on the management, which must also list ongoing relationships and pending issues (art. 1130-bis of the Italian Civil Code).

## What to check
- **Income and expenses**: do they match payments and supporting documents?
- **Allocation**: are expenses split with the correct ownership shares?
- **Reserve fund and balances**: are they shown clearly?
- **Contracts**: are service costs (cleaning, lift, garden) in line with last year?

## How to ask for clarification
Owners can inspect the supporting documents and copy them at their own expense. It is better to do so before the meeting, so you arrive with precise questions.
