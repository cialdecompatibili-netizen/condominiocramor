The annual statement consists of an accounts register, a financial summary and an explanatory note on the management, which must also list ongoing relationships and pending issues (art. 1130-bis of the Italian Civil Code).

## What to check
- **Income and expenses**: do they match payments and supporting documents?
- **Allocation**: are expenses split with the correct ownership shares?
- **Reserve fund and balances**: are they shown clearly?
- **Contracts**: are service costs (cleaning, lift, garden) in line with last year?

## How to ask for clarification
Owners can inspect the supporting documents and copy them at their own expense. It is better to do so before the meeting, so you arrive with precise questions.

## How to read it in five moves
1. **Compare with the budget**: large gaps without an explanation in the note should be clarified at the meeting.
2. **Check the cash**: the closing balance must match the bank statement of the building's account.
3. **Check the allocation**: each cost must be shared using the right thousandths table (property, staircases, lift, heating).
4. **Look at funds and works**: special funds for extraordinary works must be kept separate from ordinary management.
5. **Read the explanatory note**: it lists current relationships with suppliers and pending matters such as cases and defaulters.

## Your rights
You may consult expense vouchers at any time and copy them at your own cost (art. 1130-bis of the Civil Code). If the statement is unclear, ask for written clarification before the meeting. If it is not presented for two consecutive years, that is a serious irregularity that allows you to ask for removal.
