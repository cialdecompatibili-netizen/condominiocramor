Unanswered calls, ignored emails, no annual statement and the roof leak is still there. It is one of the most common reasons owners look for a new manager. Before removal, here are the steps in order.

## 1. Put everything in writing
Write by **certified email or registered letter**, describing the problem and giving a reasonable deadline. A dated written request is the basis of every later step. Informal messages leave no trace.

## 2. Ask for the documents you are entitled to
Every owner can ask to consult and obtain a copy, at their own expense, of registers, contracts and accounting documents. The manager must also keep the bank account in the condominium's name and let owners check its movements.

## 3. Involve the other owners
A problem raised by several people weighs more. Two owners representing one sixth of the building's value can request an extraordinary meeting.

## 4. Bring it to the meeting
At the meeting you can ask for an account of the facts, record requests in the minutes and, if needed, vote removal and the appointment of a new manager (majority of those present and at least 500 thousandths).

## 5. If that is not enough: court petition
For serious irregularities (for example the statement not approved for two consecutive years, resolutions not carried out, bank account not used) any owner can ask the court for removal. A lawyer is advisable here.

## What to record while waiting
- Date and content of every request and reply.
- Damage or costs caused by the delay (photos, quotes, invoices).
- Missed deadlines (meeting, statement, resolved works).

## When it makes sense to change
If problems keep repeating, changing costs less than waiting. The new manager collects the documentation, checks the accounts and starts with clear rules.
