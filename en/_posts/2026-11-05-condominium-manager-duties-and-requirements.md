A professional manager has precise duties set by the Civil Code.

## What they must do
- **Keep the registers**: owner records, minutes, appointment and dismissal, accounts.
- **Call the meeting** and prepare the annual statement.
- **Collect** contributions and **pay** suppliers.
- **Bank account** in the condominium's name, through which funds must pass (art. 1129 of the Italian Civil Code).
- **Carry out** the resolutions and look after the common parts.

## Requirements
Managers who are not owners must hold a secondary-school diploma, have no disqualifying convictions and attend initial and ongoing training (art. 71-bis of the implementing provisions).
