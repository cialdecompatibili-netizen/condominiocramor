A balcony edge shedding debris is not just a cosmetic problem: it can be a danger to people below. Understanding why it happens helps repair it well.

## Why they detach
Water seeps into the concrete, reaches the rebars and rusts them. Rust expands in volume and pushes the concrete, which cracks and falls off. Frost, heat and smog speed it up.

## The signs
- Exposed, rusty rebars
- Cracks along the edge
- Pieces that fall or sound hollow
- Rust-coloured stains

## How they are repaired
1. Unstable parts are removed.
2. Rebars are treated with anti-corrosion product.
3. The element is rebuilt with repair mortar.
4. A protective finish is applied.

If parts can fall, it is better to secure the area right away.
