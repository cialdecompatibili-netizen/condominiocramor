An owners' meeting decides better when it has clear information. For major works it pays to prepare a few extra documents.

## What to bring
- A report with photos of the building's condition
- A list of works, in order of priority
- Itemised, comparable quotes
- A schedule and site organisation
- Any alternatives (for example rope access or scaffolding)

## How we help
After the inspection we hand over a photo report and an itemised quote. If needed, we can present it at the meeting and answer residents' questions.

## A tip
Separate what is urgent from what can be planned: often you can proceed in phases, spreading the cost.

Votes follow the majorities set by law: for details ask your manager or a lawyer.
