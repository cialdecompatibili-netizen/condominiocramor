There is no single price: the fee depends on the building. What matters is that it is clear.

## What affects it
- **Number of units** and size of the building.
- **Services present**: lift, concierge, central heating, garden.
- **Number of meetings** required.
- **Extra activities**: extraordinary works, tax paperwork, debt recovery.

## What to demand
The fee must be itemised when the manager is appointed and renewed, on pain of nullity (art. 1129 of the Italian Civil Code). Ask for an item-by-item quote, VAT included, with a list of what is paid separately.
