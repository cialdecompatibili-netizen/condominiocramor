There are two ways to work on the outside of a building: scaffolding or rope access. There is no absolute best, it depends on the job.

## When rope access makes sense
- Localised work on a facade, cornices or balconies
- Gutters and downpipes at height
- Inspections and checks
- Urgent jobs
- Narrow pavements or spaces where scaffolding is a problem

Advantages: fast times, no assembly, less bulk and less disruption.

## When scaffolding is needed
- Extensive work across the whole facade
- Many different, long operations
- Thermal insulation and heavy works

## Choosing well
After the inspection we compare costs, times and disruption of both methods and put them in the quote, so the owners decide with the numbers in hand.
