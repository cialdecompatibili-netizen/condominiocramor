When it rains and a stain shows up on the top-floor ceiling, the first question is always: whose problem is it? The flat roof serves the whole building, so the law splits the cost.

## Who pays
For repairs or renewal of a flat roof, the Italian civil code (art. 1126) generally puts one third of the cost on whoever has exclusive use and two thirds on all the owners of the building or of the part below, in proportion to the value of their floors. Rules or agreements can differ: always check with your manager.

## What to do right away
1. Report the problem to the manager, with photos of the stains.
2. Have an inspection done to find where the water gets in.
3. Ask for an itemised quote to bring to the owners' meeting.
4. If the damage is urgent, the manager can order the essential works.

## Why not wait
Water ruins screeds, rebars and ceilings. A local repair today can become a full renewal tomorrow.
