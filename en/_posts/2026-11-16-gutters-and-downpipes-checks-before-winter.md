In autumn gutters fill with leaves and storms test the roof. A check before winter prevents leaks.

## What to check
- Gutters and downpipes free of leaves and debris
- Tiles in place
- Flashings and sealing intact
- Chimneys and ridges without cracks
- Drains of terraces and flat roofs open

## Why it matters
A blocked gutter makes water overflow onto the facade and balconies, ruining plaster and creating damp. A detached downpipe can wet the wall for months.

## How to do it safely
The check can be done with a drone or on ropes, without scaffolding. If needed, we clean and fix gutters and downpipes in the same visit.

Better before the heavy rains: it costs little and prevents bigger damage.
