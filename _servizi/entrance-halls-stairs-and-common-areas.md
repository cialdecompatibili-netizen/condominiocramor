Entrance halls, stairs and courtyards are the parts every resident uses every day. Orderly maintenance keeps them safe, clean and decorous, and adds value to the building.

## When you need it
- Flaking or damp walls
- Damaged or slippery steps and floors
- Garages, cellars and technical rooms to repair
- Courtyards with uneven paving
- Renewal of the entrance hall and stairwell

## What's included
- Painting and skim coats of halls and stairs
- Repair of steps, floors and skirting
- Masonry and plaster repairs
- Maintenance of garages, cellars and technical rooms
- Repair of courtyards and outdoor paving
- Works organised to limit disruption

## For managers and owners
Need something for the owners' meeting? After the inspection we prepare a report with photos and an itemised quote, and stay available to the manager for any clarification during and after the works.

## How we work
1. Inspection with photo report
2. Clear quote before starting
3. Works followed at every stage
4. Final documentation of the works
