Anyone going on the roof for a check or repair must be able to do so safely. Lifelines and anchor points protect workers and make future maintenance safer.

## When you need it
- The roof has no fall protection
- Existing anchors are old or unchecked
- Roof maintenance is planned
- Roof access is awkward or dangerous

## What's included
- Check of existing systems and access
- Installation of lifelines and anchor points
- Repair of hatches and access points
- Documentation of the installation
- Guidance on periodic checks

## For managers and owners
Need something for the owners' meeting? After the inspection we prepare a report with photos and an itemised quote, and stay available to the manager for any clarification during and after the works.

1. Inspection with photo report
2. Clear quote before starting
3. Works followed at every stage
