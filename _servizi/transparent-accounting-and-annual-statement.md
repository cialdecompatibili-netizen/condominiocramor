The annual statement must be clear and verifiable. Every owner should understand where the building's money goes: we prepare tidy documents with supporting papers and explained allocations.

## When you need it
- The statement is hard to read or late
- You doubt how expenses were split
- You want a more predictable instalment plan
- The previous accounts need a review
- Reserve fund and balances are unclear

## What's included
- Budget and instalment plan
- Annual statement: accounts register, financial summary and explanatory note
- Expense allocation using the ownership tables
- Personal statement for each owner
- Searchable archive of supporting documents
- Accounting review of previous years

## What you get
Clear documents, timely communication and a dedicated contact. We help you prepare every decision for the owners' meeting, with item-by-item quotes and easy-to-read reports.

## How we work
1. Meeting and review of documents
2. Written quote, item by item
3. Decision at the owners' meeting
4. Management with a transparent annual statement
