The annual statement must be clear and verifiable. Every owner should understand where the building's money goes: we prepare tidy documents with supporting papers and explained allocations.

## When you need it
- The statement is hard to read or late
- You doubt how expenses were split
- You want a more predictable instalment plan
- The previous accounts need a review
- Reserve fund and balances are unclear

## What's included
- Budget and instalment plan
- Annual statement: accounts register, financial summary and explanatory note
- Expense allocation using the ownership tables
- Personal statement for each owner
- Searchable archive of supporting documents
- Accounting review of previous years

## What you get
Clear documents, timely communication and a dedicated contact. We help you prepare every decision for the owners' meeting, with item-by-item quotes and easy-to-read reports.

## What you should know
- **What the statement is**: it consists of the accounting register, a financial summary and a short explanatory note on management, with current relationships and pending issues (art. 1130-bis).
- **Accounting register**: entries are made in chronological order, within thirty days of the operations.
- **Budget and final accounts**: at the start of the year a budget is approved; at year end the statement compares actual with planned spending.
- **Deadlines**: the statement must be presented to the meeting within one hundred and eighty days of the end of the financial year.
- **Special fund**: for extraordinary works the meeting sets up a fund equal to the cost of the works (art. 1135), to pay the contractor by progress stage without delays.
- **Allocation**: costs are shared according to thousandths and the law: for example maintenance in proportion to value, use-based costs according to use (art. 1123).

## Mistakes to avoid
- Mixing ordinary and extraordinary costs in the same table.
- Presenting a statement with no cash balances verifiable on the bank account.
- Leaving pending cases and credits against defaulters out of the explanatory note.

## How we work
1. Meeting and review of documents
2. Written quote, item by item
3. Decision at the owners' meeting
4. Management with a transparent annual statement
