Changing manager should not be a leap in the dark. We start from the documents and a clear quote, then follow the handover until the first meeting.

## When you need it
- Your current manager does not report or cannot be reached
- The fee is unclear or too high
- The mandate is about to expire
- You want more transparency and communication
- After an irregularity you want to restart in order

## What's included
- Review of the latest statement and current contracts
- Item-by-item administration quote, VAT included, extras listed
- Appointment proposal for the meeting
- Formal request of documents to the outgoing manager
- Check of cash, balances and owners' positions
- Start of management with a calendar of deadlines

## What you get
Clear documents, timely communication and a dedicated contact. We help you prepare every decision for the owners' meeting, with item-by-item quotes and easy-to-read reports.

## How we work
1. Meeting and review of documents
2. Written quote, item by item
3. Decision at the owners' meeting
4. Management with a transparent annual statement
