Changing manager should not be a leap in the dark. We start from the documents and a clear quote, then follow the handover until the first meeting.

## When you need it
- Your current manager does not report or cannot be reached
- The fee is unclear or too high
- The mandate is about to expire
- You want more transparency and communication
- After an irregularity you want to restart in order

## What's included
- Review of the latest statement and current contracts
- Item-by-item administration quote, VAT included, extras listed
- Appointment proposal for the meeting
- Formal request of documents to the outgoing manager
- Check of cash, balances and owners' positions
- Start of management with a calendar of deadlines

## What you get
Clear documents, timely communication and a dedicated contact. We help you prepare every decision for the owners' meeting, with item-by-item quotes and easy-to-read reports.

## What you should know
- **How to change**: the meeting removes or does not renew the old manager and appoints the new one by a majority of those present and at least 500 thousandths.
- **Outgoing manager's duties**: hand over all documentation about the building and each owner and carry out urgent tasks to avoid harm, with no right to extra fees (art. 1129).
- **Documents to collect**: registers (owners, minutes, appointments, accounting), statements, current contracts, bank statements, policies, system certificates, defaulters list and pending cases.
- **Bank**: the dedicated account stays the building's; signatories and online access change.
- **Tax**: the new representative must be notified to the tax agency and upcoming tax deadlines checked.
- **Suppliers**: notify the lift company, cleaners, maintenance firms and utilities, and check contracts due to expire.

## Mistakes to avoid
- Voting the change without an itemised quote from the new manager.
- Skipping a handover record listing the documents received.
- Letting weeks pass without updating account and signatories: payments stay blocked.

## How we work
1. Meeting and review of documents
2. Written quote, item by item
3. Decision at the owners' meeting
4. Management with a transparent annual statement
