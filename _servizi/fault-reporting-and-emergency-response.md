When something breaks in the common areas, speed makes the difference. The manager activates the right supplier and keeps owners informed.

## When you need it
- The lift stops or the stair lights fail
- There is a water leak in the common areas
- A cornice or balcony looks unsafe
- You need a locksmith, plumber or electrician now
- You want to know the status of a report

## What's included
- Clear channels to report problems (with photos if possible)
- Quick handling and checks
- Contact with the right supplier (plumber, electrician, lift technician)
- Urgent works to preserve the common parts
- Updates to owners on the status of the report
- Report to the meeting on urgent works

## What you get
Clear documents, timely communication and a dedicated contact. We help you prepare every decision for the owners' meeting, with item-by-item quotes and easy-to-read reports.

## How we work
1. Meeting and review of documents
2. Written quote, item by item
3. Decision at the owners' meeting
4. Management with a transparent annual statement
