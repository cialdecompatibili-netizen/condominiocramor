The flat roof protects the whole building from water. When the membrane wears out, stains appear on the top-floor ceilings and the damage grows with every rain. Redoing the waterproofing properly is the lasting solution.

## When you need it
- Stains on the ceilings below
- Cracked, lifted or blistered membrane
- Puddles from insufficient slopes
- Blocked drains and outlets
- Paving that lifts

## What's included
- Finding where the water gets in
- Removal of damaged coverings and membranes
- Screed and slope repair
- Continuous membrane with upstands
- Repositioning of drains and outlets
- New paving or heavy protection layer

## For managers and owners
Need something for the owners' meeting? After the inspection we prepare a report with photos and an itemised quote, and stay available to the manager for any clarification during and after the works.

## How we work
1. Inspection with photo report
2. Clear quote before starting
3. Works followed at every stage
4. Final documentation of the works
