When a piece of cornice falls or a balcony shows signs of failure, you cannot wait for the owners' meeting. We step in quickly to make things safe, then the final repair is planned calmly.

## When you need it
- Falling or about-to-fall debris
- Unstable balconies or balcony edges
- Roofs or tiles damaged by bad weather
- Damage after storms or strong wind
- A hazard notice from the authorities

## What's included
- Fast inspection
- Removal of dangerous parts
- Barriers, nets and temporary protection
- Rope access where needed
- Report with photos for the manager
- Quote for the final repair

## For managers and owners
Need something for the owners' meeting? After the inspection we prepare a report with photos and an itemised quote, and stay available to the manager for any clarification during and after the works.

## How we work
1. Inspection with photo report
2. Clear quote before starting
3. Works followed at every stage
4. Final documentation of the works
