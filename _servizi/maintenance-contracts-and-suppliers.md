We collect the quotes, compare them item by item and present them to the meeting. You and the owners choose the contractor; we check the works are done as promised.

## When you need it
- The facade, flat roof or roof must be redone
- You want to compare several contractors
- Written contracts and guarantees are missing
- You want someone to supervise the works
- Service contracts have expired or are too expensive

## What's included
- Inspection with licensed technicians, when needed
- Several quotes compared item by item
- Specification and written works contract
- Check of insurance, social-security certificate and company documents
- Works schedule shared with owners
- Progress monitoring and final acceptance

## What you get
Clear documents, timely communication and a dedicated contact. We help you prepare every decision for the owners' meeting, with item-by-item quotes and easy-to-read reports.

## What you should know
- **Lift**: maintenance contract and periodic inspection by an authorised body, normally every two years.
- **Fire protection**: extinguishers and hoses need periodic checks, usually every six months, and the check register must be kept.
- **Central heating**: an authorised maintainer, the system logbook and periodic efficiency checks.
- **Electrical system and earthing**: declaration of conformity and periodic checks, particularly where there are employees (concierge, in-house cleaners).
- **Contracts**: term, notice, automatic renewal, penalties and prices must be checked before signing. Automatic renewals are a frequent trap.
- **Suppliers**: always ask for company registration, DURC, liability insurance and, for work at height or with risks, the risk assessment.

## Mistakes to avoid
- Renewing the same supplier every year without comparing prices.
- Not keeping a schedule of mandatory checks: penalties and liability fall on the building.
- Paying for work without a written order and a service report.
