We collect the quotes, compare them item by item and present them to the meeting. You and the owners choose the contractor; we check the works are done as promised.

## When you need it
- The facade, flat roof or roof must be redone
- You want to compare several contractors
- Written contracts and guarantees are missing
- You want someone to supervise the works
- Service contracts have expired or are too expensive

## What's included
- Inspection with licensed technicians, when needed
- Several quotes compared item by item
- Specification and written works contract
- Check of insurance, social-security certificate and company documents
- Works schedule shared with owners
- Progress monitoring and final acceptance

## What you get
Clear documents, timely communication and a dedicated contact. We help you prepare every decision for the owners' meeting, with item-by-item quotes and easy-to-read reports.

## How we work
1. Meeting and review of documents
2. Written quote, item by item
3. Decision at the owners' meeting
4. Management with a transparent annual statement
