Cornices, balcony edges and balconies are among the most exposed parts: water reaches the rebars, rusts them and the concrete detaches. It is a safety matter for people walking below and should not wait.

## When you need it
- Concrete that crumbles or detaches
- Exposed, rusty rebars
- Cracked cornices and balcony edges
- Falling debris
- Balconies with lifting floors

## What's included
- Sounding and check of damaged parts
- Removal of unstable parts
- Anti-corrosion treatment of rebars
- Rebuilding with repair mortar
- Protective, waterproof finish
- Nets or temporary protection where needed

## For managers and owners
Need something for the owners' meeting? After the inspection we prepare a report with photos and an itemised quote, and stay available to the manager for any clarification during and after the works.
