Before voting on works, the owners need to know what is to be done and what it costs. A documented inspection, with photos and drone footage, puts everyone on the same page.

## When you need it
- The manager must prepare the owners' meeting
- Residents report damage or leaks
- You need to compare several quotes
- Periodic check of the building

## What's included
- Inspection of facades, balconies, roofs and common areas
- Drone footage where useful
- Photographic report of the issues
- Description of recommended works and priorities
- Itemised quote
- Availability to present it at the meeting

## For managers and owners
Need something for the owners' meeting? After the inspection we prepare a report with photos and an itemised quote, and stay available to the manager for any clarification during and after the works.

## How we work
1. Inspection with photo report
2. Clear quote before starting
3. Works followed at every stage
4. Final documentation of the works
