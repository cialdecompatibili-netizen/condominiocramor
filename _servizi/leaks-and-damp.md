Ceiling stains, mould and swollen plaster rarely originate where they show. Finding the real cause avoids spending on the wrong fix and seeing the problem come back.

## When you need it
- Stains and halos on ceilings and walls
- Mould and bad smells
- Plaster that swells or detaches
- Rising damp in low walls
- Water after every storm

## What's included
- Inspection and search for the entry point
- Work on terraces, facades, gutters or pipes, depending on the cause
- Targeted waterproofing and sealing
- Dehumidification and plaster restoration
- Repainting of damaged areas
- Report with photos for the manager and insurer

## For managers and owners
Need something for the owners' meeting? After the inspection we prepare a report with photos and an itemised quote, and stay available to the manager for any clarification during and after the works.

## How we work
1. Inspection with photo report
2. Clear quote before starting
3. Works followed at every stage
4. Final documentation of the works
