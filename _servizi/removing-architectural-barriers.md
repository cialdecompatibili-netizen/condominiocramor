An accessible condominium is more comfortable for everyone: older people, people with reduced mobility, families with prams. The building works for ramps, slopes and entrances are our job; the design belongs to the engineer.

## When you need it
- Steps at the entrance or on shared routes
- A request from a resident
- Existing ramps that are too steep
- Uneven outdoor paths

## What's included
- Construction of ramps and slopes
- Adjustment of thresholds and routes
- Non-slip paving
- Handrails and parapets
- Masonry works for lifts and platforms
- Coordination with the design engineer

## For managers and owners
Need something for the owners' meeting? After the inspection we prepare a report with photos and an itemised quote, and stay available to the manager for any clarification during and after the works.

## How we work
1. Inspection with photo report
2. Clear quote before starting
3. Works followed at every stage
4. Final documentation of the works
