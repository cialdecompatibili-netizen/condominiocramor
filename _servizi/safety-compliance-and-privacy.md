Lifts, systems, insurance, owners' data and cameras: many obligations, each with a deadline. A tidy calendar avoids penalties and surprises.

## When you need it
- You do not know which periodic checks have lapsed
- Certificates and logbooks must be put in order
- You want to install CCTV
- There are employees (concierge, cleaners) to manage
- Insurance is inadequate

## What's included
- Calendar of periodic checks (lift, systems, fire safety where required)
- Dealings with licensed firms and certificate checks
- Owner records and personal data
- Privacy and CCTV management
- Building and liability insurance
- Compliance as the building's tax withholding agent

## What you get
Clear documents, timely communication and a dedicated contact. We help you prepare every decision for the owners' meeting, with item-by-item quotes and easy-to-read reports.

## What you should know
- **Building register**: the manager keeps a register with owners' data, land registry data and information on the safety conditions of common parts (art. 1130).
- **Periodic checks**: lift, electrical and earthing systems, fire protection, heating. A schedule avoids penalties and liability.
- **Fire prevention**: garages, tall buildings or premises with particular systems may need specific certificates. We check case by case with a technician.
- **CCTV**: installing it on common parts requires a resolution with at least 500 thousandths (art. 1122-ter). Information signs and limited image retention are required.
- **Privacy**: owners' data is used only to manage the building. No defaulters lists on notice boards, no personal data in open chats.
- **Paperwork**: declarations of conformity, system logbooks and certificates must be kept and handed over to the next manager.

## Mistakes to avoid
- Installing cameras without a resolution or signs.
- Not knowing which certificates the building actually has: they are often discovered after an incident.
- Leaving personal data in shared sheets or group chats.
