Lifts, systems, insurance, owners' data and cameras: many obligations, each with a deadline. A tidy calendar avoids penalties and surprises.

## When you need it
- You do not know which periodic checks have lapsed
- Certificates and logbooks must be put in order
- You want to install CCTV
- There are employees (concierge, cleaners) to manage
- Insurance is inadequate

## What's included
- Calendar of periodic checks (lift, systems, fire safety where required)
- Dealings with licensed firms and certificate checks
- Owner records and personal data
- Privacy and CCTV management
- Building and liability insurance
- Compliance as the building's tax withholding agent

## What you get
Clear documents, timely communication and a dedicated contact. We help you prepare every decision for the owners' meeting, with item-by-item quotes and easy-to-read reports.

## How we work
1. Meeting and review of documents
2. Written quote, item by item
3. Decision at the owners' meeting
4. Management with a transparent annual statement
