The roof is the building's first defence. Broken tiles, loose ridges and blocked gutters let water in where it shouldn't go: a regular check avoids leaks and far more expensive repairs.

## When you need it
- Moved, broken or missing tiles
- Leaks after storms
- Blocked or detached gutters and downpipes
- Moss and vegetation on the roof
- Check before winter

## What's included
- Roof inspection, drone included
- Replacement of damaged tiles
- Repair of ridges, chimneys and flashings
- Cleaning and renewal of gutters and downpipes
- Membranes and sealing of joints and flashings
- Securing of roof access

## For managers and owners
Need something for the owners' meeting? After the inspection we prepare a report with photos and an itemised quote, and stay available to the manager for any clarification during and after the works.

## How we work
1. Inspection with photo report
2. Clear quote before starting
3. Works followed at every stage
4. Final documentation of the works
