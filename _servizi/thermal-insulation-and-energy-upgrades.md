External thermal insulation wraps the walls from outside, cuts heat loss and mould and improves comfort at home. In a condominium it is a major job that needs a design, a vote and an organised contractor.

## When you need it
- Cold walls, mould and condensation
- Very high heating bills
- A facade that needs redoing anyway
- An energy upgrade project for the building

## What's included
- Inspection and assessment of the building
- Insulation panels and skim coat
- Facade finish and painting
- Care for details: sills, reveals, corners
- Coordination with the project engineers
- Photographic documentation of each phase

## For managers and owners
Need something for the owners' meeting? After the inspection we prepare a report with photos and an itemised quote, and stay available to the manager for any clarification during and after the works.

## How we work
1. Inspection with photo report
2. Clear quote before starting
3. Works followed at every stage
4. Final documentation of the works
