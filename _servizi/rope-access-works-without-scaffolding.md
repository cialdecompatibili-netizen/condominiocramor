Rope access lets us reach facades, balconies and gutters without erecting scaffolding. For targeted jobs it is faster, keeps pavements and entrances free and reduces disruption for residents.

## When you need it
- Localised work on a facade or cornice
- Gutters and downpipes at height
- Inspections of high parts
- Urgent jobs where scaffolding is not convenient
- Narrow or hard-to-reach spaces

## What's included
- Rope access feasibility check
- Repairs of plaster and balcony edges
- Cleaning and repair of gutters and downpipes
- Sealing and small repairs at height
- Installation of anti-pigeon nets
- Photographic documentation of the work

## For managers and owners
Need something for the owners' meeting? After the inspection we prepare a report with photos and an itemised quote, and stay available to the manager for any clarification during and after the works.

## How we work
1. Inspection with photo report
2. Clear quote before starting
3. Works followed at every stage
4. Final documentation of the works
