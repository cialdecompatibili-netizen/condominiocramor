Pigeons make a mess, damage facades and cornices, and guano can be a hygiene problem. A targeted prevention job, with deterrents or nets, keeps birds away without harming the building.

## When you need it
- Guano on balconies, sills and cornices
- Pigeons nesting on terraces and attics
- Bad smells and dirt in common areas
- Cornices damaged by build-up

## What's included
- Cleaning and sanitising of dirty areas
- Installation of spikes and wire deterrents
- Protective nets for attics and recesses
- Sealing of entry points
- Rope access for high areas
- Advice on maintenance over time
