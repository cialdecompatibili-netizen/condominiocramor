Facades, insulation, systems, lifts: extraordinary works need correct resolutions, tidy documents and attention to the tax incentives in force, which change over time.

## When you need it
- The building wants a renovation project
- You want to understand which tax incentives exist
- Resolutions with the right majorities are needed
- Documents for the deductions are missing
- Technicians, contractors and accountant must be coordinated

## What's included
- Check of applicable incentives with technicians and accountants
- Meeting resolutions with the correct majorities
- Collection of documents for the deductions
- Tax compliance of the condominium
- Communication to owners on instalments and shares
- Filing for any future checks

## What you get
Clear documents, timely communication and a dedicated contact. We help you prepare every decision for the owners' meeting, with item-by-item quotes and easy-to-read reports.

## What you should know
- **Decision**: extraordinary maintenance is approved by a majority of those present and at least 500 thousandths (art. 1136). Improvements follow the rules of art. 1120.
- **Before the meeting**: collect several comparable quotes with the same specification. A single quote does not tell you whether the price is fair.
- **Works contract**: must state price, timing, penalties, progress stages, guarantees and the safety manager. The meeting sets up a special fund equal to the cost of the works (art. 1135).
- **Checking the contractor**: contribution compliance certificate (DURC), technical and professional suitability, insurance, licences for scaffolding and work at height.
- **Tax withholding**: on payments to the contractor for works the building applies a 4% withholding and pays it as withholding agent.
- **Building tax bonuses**: rates, limits and requirements change often. Before proposing a resolution we check the rules in force and rely on a technician or accountant for certifications and filings.

## Mistakes to avoid
- Voting on the basis of a single "trusted" quote.
- Hiring a contractor without checking DURC and insurance.
- Promising owners a tax bonus without checking current requirements and deadlines.
