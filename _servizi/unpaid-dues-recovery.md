When some owners do not pay, the burden falls on those who do and on suppliers. Acting early, with formal notices and a lawyer if needed, protects the building's funds.

## When you need it
- Unpaid instalments weigh on the other owners
- Suppliers are waiting to be paid
- An owner ignores reminders
- Legal action through a lawyer is needed
- You want to prevent arrears with a sustainable plan

## What's included
- Reminders and formal notices
- Agreed repayment plan, where possible
- Lawyer appointed for the payment order
- Disclosure of defaulters' data to creditors, if requested
- Suspension of separable common services where permitted
- Up-to-date list of receivables and report to the meeting

## What you get
Clear documents, timely communication and a dedicated contact. We help you prepare every decision for the owners' meeting, with item-by-item quotes and easy-to-read reports.

## How we work
1. Meeting and review of documents
2. Written quote, item by item
3. Decision at the owners' meeting
4. Management with a transparent annual statement
