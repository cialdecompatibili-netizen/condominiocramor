Many owner requests are about documents (where are the minutes? how much do I owe?). A residents' area answers them in a few clicks and frees time to actually manage the building.

## When you need it
- You want minutes and statements without calling the office
- You need an up-to-date personal statement
- You want to report a fault with a photo
- Notices about deadlines and meetings arrive late
- You have owners and tenants to keep informed

## What's included
- Digital notice board with minutes, statements and circulars
- Personal statement for each owner
- Fault reporting with photos
- Alerts for deadlines and meetings
- Always up-to-date document archive
- Access from computer and smartphone, for authorised users only

## What you get
Clear documents, timely communication and a dedicated contact. We help you prepare every decision for the owners' meeting, with item-by-item quotes and easy-to-read reports.

## How we work
1. Meeting and review of documents
2. Written quote, item by item
3. Decision at the owners' meeting
4. Management with a transparent annual statement
