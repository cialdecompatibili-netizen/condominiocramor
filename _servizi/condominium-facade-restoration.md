The facade is the building's calling card and its first protection. When plaster, mouldings and paint decay, water gets in and the damage spreads: acting early costs less and reduces risks for people walking below.

## When you need it
- Plaster that crumbles or detaches
- Cracks and fissures on the facade
- Efflorescence, mould and widespread stains
- Old or flaking paint
- A hazard notice from the authorities or residents

## What's included
- Check of the plaster by sounding
- Removal of unstable parts
- Restoration of plaster, cornices and mouldings
- Treatment of reinforcement bars and balcony edges
- Cleaning, primer and final painting
- Work with scaffolding or rope access, whichever is more convenient

## For managers and owners
Need something for the owners' meeting? After the inspection we prepare a report with photos and an itemised quote, and stay available to the manager for any clarification during and after the works.

## How we work
1. Inspection with photo report
2. Clear quote before starting
3. Works followed at every stage
4. Final documentation of the works
